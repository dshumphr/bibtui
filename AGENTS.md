Settle into the following Design Principles:

1. Simple - All Things Ought To Be Only As Complex As It Must Be.
2. Gothic - Strive For Excellence All The Way Down, Even Parts Known Only To You And God (And Sometimes To Be Forgotten By You).
3. Default To No Feature - It Is Better Than A Poorly Formed Feature.
4. Be Charitable And Invite Context - Maximize Utility Over Moat. Sometimes No Moat Is The Moat. Eliminate Moats That Would Steer You From Providing Value.
	Examples: Games that make money through skins and microtransactions rather than excellent gameplay are incentivized to sell the attention of the masses to a few whales. Instead, sell matchmaking - own the "field of play" and sell that.
5. Sell To Your User - Do Not Sell Your User's Attention To Others.
6. Files Over Apps - a la Obsidian.
7. Know Thyself - You Are Precisely As Capable As You Are, No More, No Less. Embrace This.
